Does Thurston Moore boycott Israel?

Yes. Thurston Moore has publicly supported a cultural boycott of Israel or taken a public stance against Israeli policy, according to the public record below.

What Thurston Moore said or did

Sonic Youth co-founder who cancelled his Tel Aviv solo concert in 2011 after receiving an open letter from Palestinian artists; has signed multiple Musicians for Palestine open letters calling for cultural boycott of Israel. Also signed the May 2021 Musicians for Palestine open letter calling on artists to refuse to perform at Israeli cultural institutions.
