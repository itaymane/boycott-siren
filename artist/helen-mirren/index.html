Does Helen Mirren boycott Israel?

No. Helen Mirren has performed in Israel or publicly opposed the cultural boycott, according to the public record below.

What Helen Mirren said or did

Signed the Creative Community for Peace statement rejecting the cultural boycott of Israel and led Hollywood stars in signing a letter supporting Israel's inclusion in the 2024 Eurovision Song Contest. She has also performed and attended events in Israel, declining pressure to boycott the country.
