Does Boy George boycott Israel?

No. Boy George has performed in Israel or publicly opposed the cultural boycott, according to the public record below.

What Boy George said or did

Signed the 2024 Creative Community for Peace open letter supporting Israel's inclusion in Eurovision and publicly vowed he 'won't turn his back on his Jewish friends' amid calls to boycott Eurovision; Culture Club frontman. In November 2026 he defied the cultural boycott to play two shows at Tel Aviv's Menora Mivtachim Arena, his first in Israel since 2017; the second was dedicated to Nova festival survivors. During the visit he met President Isaac Herzog and visited the Nova memorial site.
